# Brief: self serve merchant onboarding

## Context
Our card offers marketplace lists discounts from independent merchants. Today merchants email their
details and offer images to the partnerships team, who check them by hand. The backlog is weeks long and
we cannot reach new merchants at scale.

## What we want
A self serve portal where a merchant can sign up, submit business details and verification documents,
upload offers with images, and track review status. The partnerships team should review submissions in
batches, with automated checks flagging missing documents and images that break our creative guidelines.

## Constraints
- Business verification (KYB) must happen before any offer goes live.
- Image rules: minimum 1200x628 pixels, no text covering more than 20% of the image.
- Must work on mobile; most merchants are on their phones.

## Success looks like
Onboarding time falls from weeks to days, and the partnerships team handles three times as many merchants.
